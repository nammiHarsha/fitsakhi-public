FitSakhi

An experimental lifestyle accountability prototype: simple daily reminders for meals, water, movement and sleep, with quick check-ins over WhatsApp.

Current status

FitSakhi is a private prototype in controlled developer testing. At present it is used only by its developer, with made-up (synthetic) information. It has no public sign-up, no public users and no production WhatsApp number.

What exists today

  • A private application, run on the developer's own computer, that records a short made-up profile and preferences.
  • Rule-based daily plans built from an unreviewed development food list (no dietitian or clinician has reviewed it).
  • Scheduled reminders, yes / no / later check-ins and a basic daily consistency score.
  • A connection to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), using Meta's developer test number and one approved test recipient.

Only proposed (not built, not available)

  • Public availability, sign-up and login.
  • A production WhatsApp number.
  • Encryption of stored data and an independent security review.
  • Clinician or dietitian review of plans and food data.
  • Photo-based food check-ins.

Important

  • Not medical advice. FitSakhi does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.
  • Do not submit real health information. The prototype has no login and does not encrypt its stored data.
  • It uses no generative AI or external AI service: planning and scoring are fixed rules.
