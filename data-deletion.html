Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated 25 November 2026

If you have used FitSakhi, you can ask us to delete your data at any time. There is no account or self-service portal because the prototype is not public. The process is by email.

Step 1: send a request

Email 189x1a0398@gprec.ac.in with the subject "FitSakhi data deletion request" and include:

  • the made-up name you used in the prototype;
  • the last two digits of the WhatsApp number you used (please do not send the full number or any health details);
  • whether you want everything deleted (the default) or only certain items.

Step 2: confirming it is you

To avoid deleting the wrong person's data, we may ask you to confirm details only the user would know, such as the made-up name and goals you chose.

Step 3: deletion

Deleting a test profile in the prototype removes: the profile, health-related answers, food preferences and allergies, routine, consent history, plans and meal alternatives, tasks, reminders, delivery records, replies and scores, any recorded approvals and their audit entries, and the stored WhatsApp contact (your phone number).

Some technical and accounting records are kept separately and are not removed automatically: cost-accounting entries (which contain no personal data) and internal message-identifier and event records (which contain no names, phone numbers or message text). On request we also remove the technical records that can be linked to your messages, and we delete any manual archived copies of the test database that contain your data. We will reply by email to confirm when deletion is done.

What we cannot delete: data held by Meta and on your phone

Messages also exist in WhatsApp on your phone and within Meta's systems, which we do not control. You can delete the chat on your phone and use Meta's privacy tools or contact Meta: WhatsApp Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.

Stop messages immediately

Reply STOP to the FitSakhi WhatsApp number to pause reminders. This pauses messages but does not delete data, so also send the request above.

If you shared real health information by mistake

Tell us straight away, saying only that you shared real information. We will treat it as urgent. Please do not repeat the information in your message.

Questions or complaints
