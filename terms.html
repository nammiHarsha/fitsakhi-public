Terms of Service

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: FitSakhi is an experimental prototype. It is not medical advice. Use made-up information only. It may change or stop at any time.

1. What this is

FitSakhi is an experimental lifestyle accountability prototype in controlled developer testing, operated by an individual developer. It is not a public service. It may change, be interrupted or be stopped at any time.

2. Who may use it

Only adults aged 18 or over who have been invited by the developer and have read the Privacy Policy.

3. Not medical advice

  • FitSakhi is a reminder tool. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition and gives no medical, nutritional or medication advice.
  • Meal suggestions come from an unreviewed development food list. Allergy and diet filtering uses ingredient lists only and cannot account for how food is prepared, packaged or cross-contaminated. Never rely on FitSakhi to avoid an allergen.
  • Do not use FitSakhi in an emergency. If you feel unwell, speak to a doctor or contact local emergency services (in India, for example, 112).
  • Do not change any medicine, diet or exercise because of a FitSakhi message. Ask a qualified professional.

4. Testing rules

  • Give made-up information only. Do not submit real health information or anyone else's personal information.
  • Do not try to bypass the test restrictions, for example by messaging numbers that were not approved.
  • Do not use it for anything unlawful, harmful or abusive.

5. WhatsApp and Meta

Messages are carried by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, so WhatsApp's and Meta's own terms and policies also apply. Messages can be delayed, missed or not delivered. To stop messages, reply STOP.

6. As is

This is an experiment provided "as is", with no promise of accuracy or availability.

7. Stopping

You can stop at any time by replying STOP and by asking for deletion (see Data Deletion Instructions). The developer may end anyone's participation at any time.
