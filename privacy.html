Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: FitSakhi is an experimental prototype. Use made-up information only. Data is kept in a local file on the developer's computer, which has no login and is not encrypted by the application. Messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. You can ask for deletion at any time (how). This is a plain-language notice for a developer test, not legal advice, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. Who is responsible

FitSakhi is operated by an individual developer. Contact: 189x1a0398@gprec.ac.in.

2. What FitSakhi is, and what is not built

Today (developer prototype): a private application on the developer's computer that records a short profile and preferences, builds simple rule-based daily plans from an unreviewed development food list, schedules reminders, records yes / no / later replies, calculates a basic daily consistency score, sends WhatsApp messages to one approved test recipient through Meta's developer test number, and is designed to receive that person's replies during testing sessions. At present only the developer uses it.

Only proposed, not built: public sign-up and login, a production WhatsApp number, encryption of stored data, independent security review, clinician or dietitian review, and photo check-ins. This policy describes only what exists today and will be updated before any of these are built or before other people are invited.

3. Do not submit real health information

Please give made-up information only, including any condition, medication, allergy or glucose value the prototype asks about. The prototype has no login, does not encrypt its stored data, and is not designed to protect real health records. WhatsApp's rules also restrict health information (see section 5). If you think you shared real health information, email 189x1a0398@gprec.ac.in and we will prioritise removing it.

5. WhatsApp and Meta

  • Messages are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), using Meta's developer test number and one approved test recipient. Sending a test message has been verified. Replies are designed to come back the same way.
  • An outgoing reminder currently contains only a short task title and a time (for example "Breakfast (08:00)") with reply buttons.
  • Replies and delivery updates are designed to come back through a webhook that runs only during short testing sessions on the developer's computer. Real incoming WhatsApp messages have not yet been verified to reach it: so far only Meta's dashboard test notifications have. When it works, any message you send to the test number is designed to reach it; only button taps and the words STOP or PAUSE would be acted on, and other message text is ignored and not stored. Each request is checked against Meta's signature before it is processed.
  • During those sessions Meta's webhook traffic reaches the developer's computer through a temporary tunnel run by Cloudflare, Inc. (a Cloudflare Quick Tunnel). That traffic, which can include the recipient's phone number and message content, therefore passes through Cloudflare's network under Cloudflare's own terms. The tunnel is stopped when the session ends.
  • To deliver messages, the recipient's phone number and the message content and details are processed by Meta under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control Meta's handling, retention or where it processes data.
  • Meta's terms state that the WhatsApp Business Platform is not HIPAA compliant, and WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy restricts using WhatsApp to send or request health related information. This is why real health information must not be used.
  • Replying STOP is designed to pause reminders, but because the webhook runs only during testing sessions it may not be processed straight away. To stop messages reliably, email us.

Meta's own policies: WhatsApp Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy, WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.

6. Sharing, advertising, analytics and AI

  • We do not sell data and show no advertising.
  • Data is shared only with Meta and, for the temporary testing tunnel, Cloudflare, as described above, and where we are legally required to.
  • FitSakhi uses no generative AI and sends no data to any external AI service.
  • This website is static and uses no cookies, scripts, analytics or trackers, and has no forms. Its hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement; we do not receive them.

7. Your choices

Taking part is voluntary. You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent, by emailing 189x1a0398@gprec.ac.in. Deletion is explained on the Data Deletion Instructions page. Privacy complaints can be sent to the same address.

8. How long data is kept

Test data is kept until you ask us to delete it or until developer testing ends, whichever comes first. When testing ends, the test data is deleted. Manual archived copies of the test database are deleted on the same terms.

9. Security: what exists and what does not

What exists: the prototype runs on the developer's own computer and its services listen only on that computer, except that during testing sessions the webhook is made reachable through a temporary Cloudflare tunnel; webhook requests from Meta are signature-checked; access credentials are kept in memory or the developer's private environment and are not stored in the database; messages are limited to an approved test recipient.

What does not exist: the application does not encrypt its stored data, has no user login, and has had no independent security review. We make no guarantee of security. For this reason real health information must not be submitted.

10. Adults only

The prototype is for adults aged 18 or over. It must not be used by or for children.

11. Changes

This policy will be updated, and its date changed, before any proposed feature is built or before other people are invited to use the prototype.
